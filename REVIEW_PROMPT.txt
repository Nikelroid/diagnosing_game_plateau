PART TWO: BRAINSTORM, claim C11
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Here the question is not whether the paper is defensible. It is whether this is the right

  KEEP     load-bearing; say what it is carrying

  DIG      there is more here than the authors have taken; say what to measure

Be concrete. "Strengthen the motivation" is not advice. "Cut the architecture probe and use
